# Email templates

AdFlo does not send email yet, so paste these from your support address. Replace the `{{placeholders}}`.

## Welcome (after the workspace is created and the owner is invited)

**Subject:** Welcome to AdFlo, {{client_name}}

Hi {{owner_first_name}},

Your AdFlo workspace for {{client_name}} is ready. You will get a separate email from AdFlo to set your password.

To get started:
1. Sign in at {{app_url}}/login.
2. Upload your first document from Documents.
3. Ask Addy to help set up your business.

If you get stuck, reply to this email. We answer within {{response_target}} on {{support_days}}.

{{your_name}}
Aurum Ventura Enterprise LLC

## Agreement (when you send the agreement link)

**Subject:** Your AdFlo agreement, please review and accept

Hi {{owner_first_name}},

Before we turn on your workspace, please review and accept the agreement here:

{{agreement_link}}

It takes about two minutes. Accepting is recorded with your name and the date. This is a typed-name acceptance, not a certified e-signature. If anything needs a change, reply and tell us.

{{your_name}}

## Password reset (after sending a reset from the Control Center)

**Subject:** Reset your AdFlo password

Hi {{owner_first_name}},

We sent a password reset email from AdFlo to this address. The link is single use and expires, so please use it soon. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check spam, then reply and we will send another.

For your safety, we will never ask you for your password or an authenticator code.

{{your_name}}
