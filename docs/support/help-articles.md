# Help articles (v1)

## 1. Sign in
Go to the sign-in page and enter your email and password. Forgot your password? Choose Forgot password and follow the emailed link. The link works once. Still stuck? Email kyleonbiz@gmail.com and we will send a new one.

## 2. Upload a document
Open Documents, choose Upload, and pick one or more files. AdFlo reads the text in supported files so you can search it. Files without readable text (such as photos of paper) are stored but marked as not searchable. Large files may be marked unsupported.

## 3. Share a document
Open the document and choose Share. Pick an expiry, then copy the link. Anyone with the link can open it until it expires or you revoke it. Revoke from the same menu at any time.

## 4. Add a license
Open Licenses, choose Add, then enter the license name, number, issuer and expiry date. AdFlo flags licenses that are close to expiring. Attach the document from Documents. Note: records here are saved in your browser in the current release.

## 5. Ask Addy
Open Addy and describe what you need, for example "set up my business". Addy prepares drafts and asks before it changes anything. Do not give Addy passwords, card numbers, SSNs or tax IDs. Addy will refuse them.

## Contacting support
Email kyleonbiz@gmail.com. Support hours are weekdays, 9am to 5pm Eastern, excluding US holidays. We aim to reply to every request within one business day.
