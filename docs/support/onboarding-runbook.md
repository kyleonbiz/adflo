# Client onboarding runbook

Same steps, same order, every client. These match the checklist on the client page in the Control Center. Tick each step there as you finish it.

Before you start: confirm the pilot terms and price are agreed in writing, and you have the owner's name and email.

1. **Create the client** (Control Center, Clients, New). Status Trial for pilots. Set plan and seats.
2. **Workspace created.** Create the workspace and link it to the client. Open it and confirm the sample structure looks right.
3. **Owner invited.** Invite the owner's email. Confirm the invite shows pending. Send the Welcome email.
4. **Terms and agreement sent.** Create the agreement link, send it from your support address with the Agreement template. Accepted agreements appear on the client timeline.
5. **Plan and seats confirmed.** Check plan, seats and any price override and note. Confirm the same figures in the agreement or invoice.
6. **First sign-in completed.** Wait for the first sign-in (shown as last active). If none within 3 business days, email a nudge and offer a 15 minute call.
7. **Day 7 check-in.** Ask what they tried and what confused them. Log it as an internal note.
8. **Move to Active** when the pilot converts and billing is arranged.

Offboarding: set status Cancelled, send their data on request, then follow the retention policy.
