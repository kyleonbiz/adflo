# Data retention and deletion policy (draft for attorney review)

| Data | Kept while | After cancellation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace content (documents, records) | Account open | Returned on request, deleted within 30 days |
| Backups | Provider retention window | Roll off naturally; not restored except for disaster recovery |
| Share and agreement links | Until expiry | Cleaned up by the nightly job |
| Audit and security logs | 12 months | Kept for the same period |
| Billing records | As required by tax law | Kept as required |
| Addy messages | Stored with the workspace | Deleted with workspace content |

Deletion requests are logged in the Control Center, confirmed to the client in writing, and completed by an Owner.
