# Data Processing Addendum

Between the client (Controller) and AdFlo, operated by its owner (Processor), supplementing the Terms of Service.

1. **Scope.** Processor handles client workspace content only to provide AdFlo.
2. **Instructions.** Processor acts on the client's documented instructions, including product use and support requests.
3. **Confidentiality.** Staff with access are bound by confidentiality; Control Center access requires a second factor and is logged.
4. **Security.** Per-workspace isolation enforced in the database, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, regular review (see SECURITY_REVIEW.md).
5. **Sub-processors.** Listed at /subprocessors. Processor gives notice before adding one; the client may object in writing.
6. **Assistance.** Processor helps the client answer access, correction, export and deletion requests.
7. **Breach notice.** Processor notifies the client without undue delay, and within 72 hours of confirming a breach affecting client data.
8. **Return and deletion.** On termination, Processor returns data on request and deletes it within 30 days, and from backups as they roll off, unless law requires retention.
9. **Audit.** Processor provides reasonable information to show compliance, no more than once a year absent an incident.
